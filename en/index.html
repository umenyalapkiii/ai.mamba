process

so what do you do, and what does the AI do?

everything but the render. the idea, the story, the framing, the lighting, the colour, the pacing of the cut. the AI doesn't decide anything — it's a tool

can this be mixed with real footage?

yes, and it's usually stronger that way. if you've already shot something, i can add what isn't there — locations, characters, action, props — and fix whatever needs fixing

how's this different from 3D and CGI?

with 3D everything gets built by hand — models, textures, lighting, sims. that's weeks or months and a whole team. here you get to a shot far faster and changes are cheap, so you can try five ideas for what 3D would spend on one

what do you need from me to get started?

just the brief — what you want to end up with. brand guidelines, references, product footage all help, but none of it's essential. if you've got nothing, i'll come up with the look myself

how long does one film take?

far quicker than a normal shoot — there's no set, no crew and no schedule to work around. the real timing comes down to the creative. i'll give you a proper estimate once i've seen the brief

can you work to our brand guidelines?

yes. palette, type, tone of voice and what the brand stands for are set at the start and hold across every shot. no guidelines yet? i'll build the look from scratch

how much does it cost?

it's quoted per project — briefs vary far too much for a price list. it depends on how much creative work the idea actually takes, plus length, number of scenes, whether you need voice, cutdowns for different platforms, and what rights you want

who owns the finished film?

we agree the rights with each client individually and put it in writing before anything starts

can i run it as paid advertising or on TV?

yes. just tell me upfront where it's going — paid social, TV, organic, outdoor, marketplaces — because that decides both the deliverables and the rights

got a project?

  1. you tell me what you need
  2. i come back with what's possible, timing and cost
  3. we agree the terms, and i get started
